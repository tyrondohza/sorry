Thank You For Everything

I am writing this to tell you how sorry I am for what I did to you. Every day I promised you that I would never leave you, but I walked away when you needed me the most.

You told me every day that I needed to work on my communication, but I never really listened.

You fought for us every time, and when I needed someone to talk to, I knew I could always count on you. But I could not return that same comfort or support.

I made you suffer, and sometimes I made you think it was your fault, when it was never yours.

I just want to say that every moment spent talking to you was one of the best parts of my life.

I should have been stronger like you and fought for us, but I was too ashamed, and it kept eating away at me knowing I lost the best person I ever had.

This was my best relationship and the longest one I have ever had.

I never believed in love until you came into my life and changed me. You showed me the kind of love and effort I was supposed to give.

Sometimes I even wonder what I did to deserve you, because the way you cared for me was something I had never experienced before.

I even left before your birthday, before the plans we had made for so long, and I could not stand up and be the partner you deserved.

But I want you to know that I will always cherish the memories we shared.
